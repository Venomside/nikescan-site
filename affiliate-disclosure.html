Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 10 August 2026

The short version

When NikeScan launches, some of the links it shows to retailers may be affiliate or publisher links. If you follow one of those links and make a qualifying purchase, NikeScan may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. You pay the same price you would pay by going to that retailer directly.

What is happening today

NikeScan is in development. The service is not live, and no affiliate links are published anywhere on this site at present. This page is published in advance so that the commercial model is clear before any link is ever shown.

How affiliate links would work

  • An affiliate link is an ordinary link to a retailer that carries a tracking reference identifying NikeScan as the source of the visit.
  • If a purchase is made after following that link, the retailer or its affiliate network may pay NikeScan a commission on that sale.
  • The commission is paid by the retailer out of its own margin. It is not added to your price, and it does not affect your consumer rights, delivery or returns with that retailer.
  • Your contract for any purchase is with the retailer, not with NikeScan.

Our commitments

  • Affiliate and publisher links will be disclosed clearly wherever they appear, not buried in a footer.
  • Commission arrangements will not be used to distort how products are compared or presented.

Independence

NikeScan is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or otherwise associated with any brand, retailer, marketplace or affiliate network. All trade marks remain the property of their respective owners.

Changes to this disclosure

NikeScan is still in development, so this disclosure will be updated before any affiliate link goes live. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
