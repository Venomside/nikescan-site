Privacy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

What this notice covers

This notice covers this website only: the pages published at this address describing NikeScan while it is in development. NikeScan is not live, so there is no service behind these pages.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies of its own.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising, remarketing or tracking scripts.
  • It has no contact form, newsletter form, sign-up or waiting list, so it collects no email addresses or other personal details from visitors.
  • It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers.
  • It does not build a profile of you, and it has nothing about you to sell or share.

Hosting

These pages are served by GitHub Pages, a service operated by GitHub, Inc. As with any web host, GitHub's servers process ordinary technical request information (such as your IP address, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent string) in order to deliver the page and to protect the service from abuse. That processing is carried out by GitHub under its own terms, and NikeScan does not receive, store or have access to those server logs.

GitHub describes what it collects for this purpose in its privacy statement.

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub's privacy statement. When NikeScan launches it is expected to link to retailers and other third parties, and some of those links may be affiliate or publisher links, as described in the affiliate disclosure. Once you follow a link away from NikeScan, the privacy notice and cookie practices of that other site apply, not this one.

Your rights

UK data protection law gives you rights over personal data held about you, including the right to ask what is held, to have it corrected or erased, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Because this site collects no personal data from visitors, there is currently nothing held about you for those rights to apply to.

Changes to this notice

NikeScan is in development. If the service later collects personal data, this notice will be replaced with a fuller one setting out what is collected, why, on what legal basis, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights, and that will be published before any such collection begins. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
